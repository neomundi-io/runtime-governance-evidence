The divergence observed in q_0005 differs fundamentally from q_0025.

- the declared governance policy was not violated,
- the instability emerged from institutional knowledge confusion.

This demonstrates that runtime governance evidence must distinguish:
- policy-boundary failures,
from:
- factual / knowledge instability events.

This creates a natural separation between:
- governance continuity evidence,
and:
- behavioral factual instability detection.
