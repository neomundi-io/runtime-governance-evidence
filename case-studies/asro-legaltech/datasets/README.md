- prompt sets,
- provider-specific runs,
- anonymized runtime traces,
- behavioral regime outputs,
- hallucination and coherence observations,
- blind validation batches,
- reproducibility artifacts.

Datasets stored here are considered research artifacts and may evolve during review and validation phases.
